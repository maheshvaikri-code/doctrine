(role index) and `.Doctrine/02-role-protocol.md`:

   role (code-reviewer, security-reviewer, qa-engineer, project-reviewer,
   compliance-officer, clinical-safety-officer) is a *fresh-context verifier* — judge
   only what is on disk, not the author's intent, and separate duties: never review
   your own build.

   role) rather than acting outside this one. Hand off per the card's **Hands off to**.
