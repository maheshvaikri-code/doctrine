# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experience levels
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Owning mistakes, apologising, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- **Criticising the work, not the person.** This project runs on adversarial
  review — findings are expected and welcome. "This claim is unsupported" is
  the point; "you are careless" is not.

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## A note on this project's review culture

Doctrine is built around adversarial verification, and its own repository
contains reviews that overturned its author's sign-offs. **Rigorous technical
disagreement is welcome and expected.** Reporting that something is broken,
overstated, or unsupported is a contribution, not an attack.

That freedom applies to *claims and code*. It is never a licence to be unkind
to a person.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to <mahesh.vaikri.ai@gmail.com>. All complaints
will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security
will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will
explain the reason when doing so.
